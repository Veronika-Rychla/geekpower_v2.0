## 📖 History of Google

Tento text tě seznámí s historií společnosti Google, od jejích počátků jako studentského projektu až po její současnou roli globálního technologického lídra. Při čtení se zaměř na klíčové události a milníky, které formovaly vývoj společnosti. Sleduj, jak se z malého projektu v garáži stala jedna z nejvlivnějších společností na světě, a jak její zakladatelé, Larry Page a Sergey Brin, přispěli k revoluci ve způsobu, jakým vyhledáváme a používáme informace.

> Google is one of the most famous companies in the world. Its story began in 1996 when Larry Page and Sergey Brin, two PhD students at Stanford University, started working on a research project. They were interested in search engines and how to organize the world's information. Together, they created a new search algorithm that was better than anything available at the time.
>
> In 1998, Page and Brin decided to start their own company. They called it Google, a play on the word "googol," which is a very large number (1 followed by 100 zeros). They set up their first office in a garage in Menlo Park, California. During this time, they were working hard to improve their search engine and attract users.
>
> Google quickly became popular because it provided more accurate and faster search results than other search engines. By 2000, Google was the largest search engine in the world. The company continued to grow, adding new features and services. In 2004, Google went public, selling shares of the company to the public for the first time. This made Page and Brin very wealthy.
>
> In the following years, Google expanded into many new areas. It created services like Gmail, Google Maps, and Google Earth. The company also bought other companies, including YouTube in 2006. Google was always looking for new ways to innovate and improve.
>
> In 2015, Google restructured itself. It created a new parent company called Alphabet Inc. Google became a subsidiary of Alphabet, allowing the company to manage its many different projects more effectively. Page and Brin continued to lead the company, but they focused more on the big picture and less on day-to-day operations.
>
> Google's history shows how two students with a big idea can change the world. Their journey from a garage in Menlo Park to a global technology giant is truly remarkable. Google continues to influence how we find and use information every day.
