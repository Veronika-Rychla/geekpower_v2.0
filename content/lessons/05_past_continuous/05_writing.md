## 📝 Writing

Napiš krátký text o tvých raných zkušenostech s počítači. Jaká byla první počítačová hra, kterou si pamatuješ, že jste hrál/a? Měli jste doma počítač, když jste byli děti? Pamatuješ si první okamžik, kdy jsi použil/a internet? Jaká byla tvá oblíbená aktivita na počítači jako dítě nebo teenager?

> The first computer game I played was "Prince of Persia." I was probably around seven years old when I first played it on our school's computer. I was so fascinated by the game that I often stayed after school just to get a few extra minutes of playtime. The game was exciting it felt like a real adventure to me.
>
> At home, we had a computer that my dad bought when I was about ten years old. It was a huge desktop with a noisy modem. We kept it in the living room and everyone had to take turns using it. My mom was using it for work, and my dad was exploring early versions of email. My sister and I mostly used it for games and school projects. Having a computer at home was a big deal back then, and we treated it like a precious object.
>
> I vividly remember the first time I used the internet. I was so excited to hear the dial-up modem connecting. I spent hours exploring different websites and learning new things. As a teenager, my favourite activity on the computer was playing online games with friends. Those early internet days were full of wonder and discovery for me.

🖋 I was so excited when I first used the internet...
🖋 As a child/teenager, my favourite activity on the computer was...
