## 📖 What is BI?

Při čtení tohoto článku se zaměř na pochopení účelu Business Intelligence a jeho spojení s IT. Sleduj, jaké nástroje a technologie se v BI používají, jak BI pomáhá zlepšovat rozhodování a optimalizovat firemní procesy. Všímej si také, jak BI umožňuje objevování nových příležitostí na trhu a proč je důležité efektivně využívat BI nástroje.

> Business intelligence, often called BI, is a technology-driven process that helps companies make better decisions. It involves collecting, analysing, and presenting data. This data comes from different sources, like company records, customer feedback and market trends. BI helps executives and managers understand what is happening in their business so they can plan for the future.
>
> BI uses various tools and technologies to turn raw data into useful information. For example, data mining is a process that finds patterns in large data sets. Predictive analysis uses these patterns to predict future outcomes. With these tools, businesses can find out what might happen next and prepare for it.
>
> One of the main benefits of BI in IT is better decision-making. By providing clear and accurate information, BI helps managers make informed choices. For example, if a company sees that a certain product is selling well, they might decide to invest more in that product. On the other hand, if sales are dropping, they might need to change their strategy.
>
> BI also helps companies improve their processes. By analysing data, businesses can identify areas for improvement. For instance, if a company sees that a certain process is taking too long, they can look for ways to speed it up. This not only saves time but also reduces costs.
>
> Another important aspect of BI is discovering new opportunities. By looking at data from different angles, businesses can find new markets to enter or new products to develop. For example, if data shows that a certain demographic is interested in a type of product, the company can create targeted marketing campaigns to reach that group.
>
> In summary, business intelligence is a powerful tool in the IT world. It helps companies make better decisions, improve their processes, and find new opportunities. By using BI tools and technologies, businesses can stay competitive and achieve their goals. Whether it's through data mining, predictive analysis, or other BI techniques, the key is to use the information effectively to drive success.
