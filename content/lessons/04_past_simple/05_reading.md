## 📖 Reading

Tento text tě ještě jednou provede historií internetu od jeho počátků až po současnost. Při čtení se zaměř na klíčové události, které formovaly vývoj internetu, jako je vznik ARPANETu, vývoj protokolů TCP/IP, vynález World Wide Webu a popularizace internetových prohlížečů. Všímej si také důležitých osobností, které měly zásadní vliv na rozvoj internetu. Na závěr přemýšlej o tom , jak se internet stal nedílnou součástí našeho každodenního života.

> [!tip] Stejné téma z jiného úhlu
> Možná si říkáš, proč je čtení i poslech na stejné téma. Je to z toho důvodu, že nám často může k pochopení tématu pomoci uchopit ho z jiného úhlu a třeba jen změnit vypravěče. Pokud si tedy nevíš rady s nějakým technickým konceptem, možná je to jen proto, že ti jedno konkrétní vysvětlení nesedlo a musíš se poohlédnout po jiném.

> The history of the Internet begins with the creation of ARPANET in the late 1960s. ARPANET, or the Advanced Research Projects Agency Network, was developed by the United States Department of Defense. Its main goal was to enable secure and reliable communication between various research institutions and military sites. The first message was sent over ARPANET in 1969 from a computer at UCLA to another computer at Stanford.
>
> During the 1970s, ARPANET continued to grow, connecting more universities and research centres across the United States. One of the key innovations was the Transmission Control Protocol (TCP) and Internet Protocol (IP), developed by Vinton Cerf and Robert Kahn in the mid-1970s. These protocols allowed different networks to communicate with each other.
>
> The 1980s brought significant advancements and expansion of the internet beyond academic and military use. The National Science Foundation (NSF) played a crucial role by establishing NSFNET, a network that connected various supercomputing centers across the country. This development significantly increased the internet's capacity and reach. Additionally, the introduction of domain names in 1984 made it easier for users to navigate the growing network. This decade also saw the first commercial email services, showing the internet's potential for business and personal communication.
>
> In 1991, a major breakthrough occurred with the invention of the World Wide Web by British computer scientist Tim Berners-Lee. Working at CERN, the European Organization for Nuclear Research, Berners-Lee developed a system of hypertext documents accessible through the internet. This innovation made it possible for users to easily access and share information online, using web browsers. The first web browser, called World Wide Web, was soon followed by more user-friendly versions like Mosaic, which played an important role in popularizing the web among the general public.
>
> The mid to late 1990s witnessed explosive growth of the internet as it became more accessible to everyday users. Companies like Netscape and Microsoft developed web browsers that made it easier for people to explore the web. This period also saw the rise of internet service providers (ISPs) like AOL and CompuServe, which offered dial-up connections to millions of households. The dot-com boom of the late 1990s highlighted the internet's potential for commerce, leading to the creation of many online businesses and services.
>
> Entering the 21st century, the internet continued to evolve quickly. Broadband connections replaced dial-up, providing faster and more reliable access. Social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube changed how people communicate and share content online. Mobile internet and smartphones further expanded the internet's reach, making it an integral part of daily life. Today, the internet connects billions of devices worldwide, enabling instant communication, information sharing, and countless other activities that were unimaginable just a few decades ago.
